ABOUT FADUM

Stories are everything.

Originally from Nigeria and now based in Atlanta, Fadum is an actor drawn to stories that challenge, move, and connect with people.

Curious by nature and always looking for something new to explore, he brings authenticity, energy, and a willingness to fully step into every character he plays.

From television and film to new forms of storytelling, Fadum is building a career centered around memorable characters and meaningful stories.
